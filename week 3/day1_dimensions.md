### **3. Calculate Dimensions for the Flat Panel Photobioreactors (PBRs)**

**Goal:** Determine the practical physical dimensions and the number of individual PBR panels needed.

**Total Target Volume for Flat panel:**
Calculation: 15,600 L * 90% = **14,040 L or 14.04 m^3**
    
**Assumed Width (W) for a single Flat panel:** **15cm or (0.15m)**.

*Reasoning:* This narrow width is crucial for optimal light penetration throughout the algae culture, minimizing self-shading and ensuring efficient photosynthesis occurs.

**Assumed Height (H) for a single Flat panel:** **2.5m**.

*Reasoning:* This height balances efficient vertical space utilization with practical considerations for structural stability, material availability, and ease of access for construction and maintenance.

**Action:** Now, deciding on a **practical Length (L)** for a single flat panel module. This is a design choice that will make the panel manageable for manufacturing, transport, and installation.

**Note:**

  **Assumed Length (L) for a single PBR panel:** **1.2m**.
  
  *Reasoning:* This length is chosen to create a manageable module size that is practical for [e.g., manufacturing, transport, installation, or fitting into a typical space]."

**Action:** With chosen W, H, and now L, calculate the resulting **Volume of a Single PBR Panel**.

**Note:**

    **Calculating the Volume of a Single Flat Panel:**
    
    Formula: Volume (m³) = W (m) * H (m) * L (m)

    Calculation: 0.15m * 2.5m * 1.2m = **0.450 m^3 = 450 Liters**
    
    Thus, a single PBR panel with dimensions of ** 0.15 x 2.5 x 1.2 meters** would have a resulting volume of **450 Liters**."

**Action:** Calculating the **Total Number of Flat Panels** needed to meet the overall PBR section volume.

**Note:**

    **Number of Flat Panels Required:**
    
    Formula: `Number of Panels = (Total Target PBR Volume) / (Volume of a Single PBR Panel)`
    
    Calculation: 14,040 L / 450 L/panel = **31.2 panels = 32 panels**
    
    Therefore, **32 Flat panels** would be required to meet the PBR section's total volume target.

 **Action:** Reflect on the overall physical footprint implications of this number of panels
 
 **Note:**
 
    **Initial Footprint Reflection for PBRs:**
    
    Arranging these 32 panels (each 1.2 meters long) would require a significant linear space if placed in a single line. 
    
    This calculation highlights the necessity for an **efficient modular layout** – perhaps arranging panels in multiple rows with walkways, or considering stacked configurations – to minimize the overall footprint and and ensure adequate space for light, air circulation, and maintenance access within a community setting."

### **4. Calculate Dimensions for the Airlift Column(s)**

**Goal:** Determining the practical physical dimensions for my airlift column(s).

**Action:** Calculating the total volume specifically for the Airlift Column section based on the percentage

 **Note:**
    **Total Target Volume for Airlift Column(s):**
    Calculation: 15,600 L * 10% = **1,560 L or 1.56m^3**
    
  **Action:** Making a practical assumption for the **Diameter (D)** of a single airlift column.
 **Note (Diameter):**
    **Assumed Diameter (D) for the single airlift column:** **0.8 meters**.
    
    *Reasoning:* This size allows for efficient mixing, effective gas exchange (CO2 absorption), and proper liquid circulation for the algae culture while remaining a manageable physical component for construction and integration with the Flat Panel.

**Action:** Calculating the **Height (H)** of a single airlift column based on my chosen Diameter and the airlift's total volume.
**Note:**
    **Required Height (H) for the single Airlift Column:**
    Formula: `H = Volume (m³) / (π * (D/2)²) `
    Calculation: 1.56 / (3.14159 * (0.8/2)²) = **3.1m**
    Thus, the single airlift column with a diameter of **0.8 meters** and a height of **3.1 meters** would provide the necessary volume.

 **Action:** Confirming the number of airlift columns that will be used. 
 **Note:**
    **Number of Airlift Columns:** I will be using **1 central airlift column** to efficiently serve the entire PBR system, integrating its mixing and gas exchange functions.
